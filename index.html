What is Scout Value?

Advertised Value is the headline amount a company promotes. Scout Value is Bonus Scout's conservative estimate of the offer's practical value to a typical eligible user.

Cash equivalenceCash is valued more highly than bonus bets, credits, discounts or free trials.
Capital burdenOffers requiring large deposits receive a larger discount than low-cost offers.
TimeLong qualification or payout periods reduce practical value.
Difficulty & riskComplex steps, wagering risk, investment requirements and renewal risk reduce value.

Scout Value is an estimate—not a guarantee of profit, resale value, or cash received. The methodology can be refined as Bonus Scout grows.
